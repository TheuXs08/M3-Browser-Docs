M3 Browser respects your privacy.

1. Data Collection
M3 Browser does not operate servers that collect or store users' browsing activity or personal information.

When you visit websites, those websites may receive information necessary to provide their services, including your IP address, browser information, cookies, and other technical data.

2. Permissions
M3 Browser may request access to the following device features:

• Camera: For websites that require camera access.
• Microphone: For websites that require microphone access.
• Location: For websites that request location services.
• Files and media: For downloading, uploading, and selecting files.

These permissions are used to provide browser functionality and are subject to your approval.

3. Local Data Storage
M3 Browser may store information locally on your device, including:

- Homepage and search engine preferences.
- Browser settings.
- Cookies and website data.
- Web storage created by websites.
- Browsing history and cached content.

This information remains on your device and is not uploaded to servers operated by the M3 Browser project.

You can manage or clear locally stored browser data through the available browser settings.

4. Third-Party Websites
M3 Browser provides access to third-party websites and services.

These websites may independently collect, store, or process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

M3 Browser does not control or assume responsibility for the privacy practices of third-party services.

5. Updates and Diagnostics
M3 Browser may communicate with external services to check for application updates or provide requested functionality.

For example, update checks may retrieve version information from the project's GitHub repository.

6. Changes to This Policy
This Privacy Policy may be updated as M3 Browser evolves. Changes will be published through the project's documentation repository.

7. Contact
For questions, bug reports, or feature requests, visit the official GitHub Issues page:

https://github.com/TheuXs08/M3-Browser/issues
